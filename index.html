Heart rate
Not connected

Garmin: turn on Broadcast Heart Rate on your watch, then tap Connect. Works in Chrome on Android. On iPhone, record on the watch and import the GPX afterwards.

  • Outing → Garmin Connect: Recap → Export GPX → connect.garmin.com → Import Data.
  • Route → watch: during a trip tap “Route to watch”, import it in Garmin Connect as a Course, then Send to Device.
  • Watch → here: export GPX/TCX from Garmin Connect and import it in Activity.
